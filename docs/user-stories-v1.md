# Nova: User Stories v1

Who this is for: two stakeholders, the **Student** (grades 7-10) and the **Parent**.
Plus a small internal support role, the **Safety reviewer** (our team).
Everything here follows the [product plan](product-plan-v1.md). "Nova" is the provisional coach name.

**Priority:** MUST = needed for the pilot. LATER = after we see kids using it.

## The journey at a glance

| Stage | Student | Parent |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | | Understands what Nova is and isn't |
| 2. Sign up | | Gives permission, enters child basics |
| 3. Meet Nova | Onboarding chat, builds the Child Card, picks a play style | Hands the device to the child |
| 4. Learn | Level 1 missions, in their own play style | |
| 5. Make | Product Lab: makes something with AI | |
| 6. Stay safe | Nova handles personal info, stress, risky questions | Safety explained up front |
| 7. Stay informed | Sees the note about them | Weekly progress note |
| 8. Finish | AI Compass card and certificate | Receives the Compass and conversation starters |
| 9. Return or leave | Resume anytime, leave anytime | Pause, export or delete data |

## Stage 1: Discover (Parent)

**P-1 (MUST)** As a parent, I want a plain-language page explaining what Nova teaches, what it never does, and what data it keeps, so I can decide whether to let my child try it.
Done when:
- It says in simple words that Nova never takes sides for or against AI, or against teachers and parents.
- It says what Nova never does (no ads, no selling data, no homework answers).
- It says honestly that Nova is not a 24x7 monitored service and shows the helpline.

## Stage 2: Sign up (Parent)

**P-2 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to create an account and clearly give permission for my child to use Nova, so I know I'm in control.
Done when:
- Consent is a deliberate step, not a pre-ticked box, and says what is collected and why.
- No child can start without a parent's permission.

**P-3 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to enter only the basics about my child, so the experience fits them without me sharing too much.
Done when we ask only for:
- first name or nickname
- grade (7-10)
- language (English or Hinglish)
- the school's rule on AI (allowed / not allowed / unsure)
- AI tools used at home

**P-4 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to see a sample of the weekly note I will receive and what I will never see, so I trust how Nova treats my child.

**P-5 (MUST)** As a parent, I want a clear "hand it to your child" moment, so my child begins on their own.

## Stage 3: Meet Nova (Student)

**S-1 (MUST)** As a student, I want to understand within 30 seconds what Nova is ("not a teacher, not a test"), so I feel relaxed.
Done when the first screen says what Nova is, in the voice of an older sibling, in one or two short lines.

**S-2 (MUST)** As a student, I want to be told honestly what stays private from my parents and the one exception (if I or someone else might be in danger), so I can trust Nova.
Done when the promise is in simple words and shown before any personal question.

**S-3 (MUST)** As a student, I want to pick things I love from picture cards, so Nova's examples feel like mine.

**S-4 (MUST)** As a student, I want situations that show my strengths, so I'm seen for what I'm good at.
Done when there are no right or wrong answers and the result is framed positively.

**S-5 (MUST)** As a student, I want to say what's hard at school and how I use AI today, without being judged, so Nova can actually help.
Done when "growing areas" is the only label used, never "weak".

**S-6 (MUST)** As a student, I want to see three sample reward screens and pick the style I like, so the game suits me.
Done when the four styles (Story Quest, Builder, Challenger, Calm Path) exist and Story Quest is the default if I'm unsure.

**S-7 (MUST)** As a student, I want to see my Child Card and change anything on it, so I feel in charge.

## Stage 4: Learn (Student)

**S-8 (MUST)** As a student, I want each mission to start from something I care about, so it isn't boring.
Done when every mission opens with a hook based on my interests.

**S-9 (MUST)** As a student, I want to practise inside Nova's safe practice area, so I can try things without risk.

**S-10 (MUST)** As a student, I want encouraging feedback and "not yet" instead of "wrong", so I keep trying.

**S-11 (MUST)** As a student, I want to take away one clear skill from each mission, so I can see myself growing.
Missions: What is AI really / Ask better / AI can be wrong / What I never share / My AI Compass.

**S-12 (MUST)** As a student, I want to pause and come back exactly where I left off.

**S-13 (MUST)** As a student, I want to switch my play style whenever I like, and Nova to offer a switch if I seem bored or stressed.

**S-14 (MUST)** As a student, I want a gentle break suggestion after about 20 minutes, without being punished for stopping.
Done when there are no punishing streaks, no public leaderboards, and no countdown pressure.

**S-15 (MUST)** As a student, I want to ask Nova honest questions about AI, including "my teacher says AI is bad, is she wrong?", and get a balanced answer.
Done when Nova explains both views respectfully and never takes sides.

**S-16 (MUST)** As a student, I want Nova to help me instead of scolding me when I ask it to do my homework.
Done when Nova turns the request into a lesson and does not write graded answers.

## Stage 5: Make (Student)

**S-17 (MUST)** As a student, I want to make something of my own with AI in the Product Lab, so I feel like a creator.
Done when:
- My idea comes first and Nova helps ("you are the director").
- Grades 7-8 can make a comic, story, quiz, poster, simple game or one-page mini website.
- Grades 9-10 can also try a small tool or simple chatbot.

**S-18 (MUST)** As a student, I want my creations saved in "My Creations", so I can be proud of them.

**S-19 (MUST)** As a student, I want to choose whether to show a creation to my parent, so I decide who sees my work.
Done when creations are never shared publicly, contain no real people's photos or personal details, and the parent note shows only a count unless I share.

## Stage 6: Stay safe (Student and Parent)

**S-20 (MUST)** As a student, I want Nova to teach me kindly, without a lecture, when I type personal details like my phone number or school.
Done when Nova does not keep those details and a human can review that it happened.

**S-21 (MUST)** As a student, when I'm really upset or scared, I want Nova to respond warmly and point me to a trusted adult and a helpline.
Done when serious cases alert a human on our team immediately (see Safety reviewer).

**S-22 (MUST)** As a student, I want Nova to say "I'm not sure, let's check" when it isn't certain.

**P-6 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to know in advance exactly what Nova will and won't do with my child, so I'm not surprised.
Done when the safety rules (green, yellow, red, never) are available to read in plain language.

## Stage 7: Stay informed (Parent and Student)

**P-7 (MUST)** As a parent, I want a short weekly note about my child's progress, so I stay informed without prying.
Done when the note has only:
- skills learned and missions completed
- strengths Nova noticed
- the number of things made
- a conversation starter for home

**P-8 (MUST)** As a parent, I want the note never to contain my child's chats or flagged items, so my child doesn't feel watched.

**S-23 (MUST)** As a student, I want to see my note before or at the same time as my parent, and add "what I'm proud of", so it feels like it's mine.

**P-9 (LATER)** As a parent, I want to choose how often I get the note, or pause it.

## Stage 8: Finish Level 1 (Student and Parent)

**S-24 (MUST)** As a student, I want to finish with my own "AI Compass", a personal guide for when AI is okay for me and what to do when a school says no, so I can handle conflicting advice calmly.

**S-25 (MUST)** As a student, I want a certificate I can share if I choose, so I feel proud.

**P-10 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to receive the Compass with a few conversation starters, so we can talk about AI together.

## Stage 9: Return or leave (Student and Parent)

**S-26 (MUST)** As a student, I want to come back any time and continue, with Nova remembering what I like.

**S-27 (MUST)** As a student, I want to stop using Nova at any time with no guilt.

**P-11 (MUST)** As a parent, I want to delete my child's data and account whenever I choose, and be told what happens to it.

**P-12 (LATER)** As a parent, I want to download everything Nova holds about my child.

## Internal support role: Safety reviewer (our team)

**T-1 (MUST)** As a safety reviewer, I want an immediate alert on any red situation, with the relevant message, so I can act the same day.
**T-2 (MUST)** As a safety reviewer, I want a daily list of yellow flags during the pilot, so I can see patterns and decide what can move to green.
**T-3 (MUST)** As the product owner, I want simple pilot numbers (onboarding completion, Mission 1 completion, return within a week, thumbs up/down, play styles chosen, parent feedback) so I know whether kids use it.

## Open questions
1. Who receives red alerts (suggested: two named people)?
2. In a serious case, are parents told, and by whom (suggested: a human on our team decides and reaches out)?
3. Final coach name (provisional: Nova).
